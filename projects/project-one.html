Event and Program Coordinator

The 4th Annual Immigrants in Tech Summit

I supported the Immigrants in Tech Summit, a 500+ attendee, multi-venue event hosted at the Royal Ontario Museum, contributing to both its communications strategy and on-the-ground execution. In the lead-up to the event, I helped drive outreach and engagement through digital marketing efforts, designing and publishing branded LinkedIn content and promotional materials in Canva to maintain a cohesive and professional visual identity across all campaign touchpoints. I also managed ongoing communication with attendees, speakers, and industry partners through coordinated email outreach, ensuring clear, timely, and consistent engagement throughout the planning process.

Rebrand Design Project

Website Design

Information Architecture; Navigation; Wireframes; Hi-fi & Low-fi Prototype

Designed a structured single-page website prototype exploring the history of Gaza, focusing on making complex information more accessible through clear organization and user-centered design. The project included developing both a non-modified and revised information architecture, supported by tree diagrams, as well as defining an intuitive navigation system.

I created initial wireframe sketches for the homepage, followed by low-fidelity wireframes for both desktop and mobile in Adobe XD, and developed high-fidelity interface mockups for key pages. This process emphasized usability, clear content hierarchy, and effective visual communication across desktop and mobile interfaces.
